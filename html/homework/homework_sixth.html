Fitness Routine Card

Hello! This is my Fitness Routine Card, and I will explain my routine on this page.


This first exercise you should do, is starting by doing some high knees and also do some jumping jacks to get ready for the excercise.


First Exercise: Start by running on the spot, and try to make sure your knees are high.


Second Exercise: Lay down on your back, and try to do at least 10 sit-ups, and after try lifting your legs in the air.


Third Exercise: Start to stretch your back, arms, and legs, so you can perform better.


Fourth Exercise: Lastly, go out for some jogging outside (unless it is terrible weather).



Fitness Tip: A good routine includes exercise, rest, healthy food, and consistency.
